COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE INITIATIVES

Healthcare Initiatives & Services

Providing accessible medical care, preventive screening, voluntary blood camps, and critical care support to underprivileged families.

Patient Welfare

Assisting underprivileged patients with necessary medical attention, hospital guidance, care coordination, and post-treatment recovery support.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Hospital visitation & guidance support
  • Medical care coordination for underprivileged families
  • Post-treatment patient recovery assistance

Blood Donation

Organizing voluntary blood donation camps and building community awareness for emergency medical requirements across hospitals and clinics.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Voluntary community blood donation drives
  • Partnerships with certified blood banks
  • Emergency blood donor registry support

Health Check-ups

Conducting free preventive health screening camps, eye check-ups, and maternal health consultations for local communities.

Key Focus Areas:
  • General preventive medical check-up camps
  • Maternal & child health screening
  • Diagnostic & eye screening consultations

Medical Support

Providing essential medicines, equipment aid, and financial guidance for critical treatments to needy individuals.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Essential medicine distribution support
  • Medical equipment assistance for senior citizens
  • Guidance for government health scheme benefits
