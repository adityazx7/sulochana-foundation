Social Welfare & Livelihood Projects

Supporting women empowerment, skill development, food security, community welfare, and emergency assistance for needy families.

Women's Empowerment

Fostering economic self-reliance through vocational skill training, financial literacy, and self-help group mentoring for women.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Tailoring, handicraft & skill workshops
  • Financial literacy & savings guidance
  • Self-help group setup & livelihood support

Community Welfare

Building inclusive community infrastructure, civic engagement projects, and shelter home support for vulnerable citizens.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Community center & shelter home assistance
  • Civic rights & entitlement awareness
  • Senior citizen welfare programs

Food Distribution

Conducting ration kit distribution and nutritious meal drives for needy families, homeless individuals, and disaster victims.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Dry ration kit distribution drives
  • Nutritious food relief during emergencies
  • Hunger alleviation in vulnerable pockets

Support for Needy People

Distributing essential clothing, blankets, hygiene kits, and emergency aid to marginalized socio-economic groups.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Winter clothing & blanket distribution
  • Hygiene & dignity kit distribution
  • Immediate emergency relief assistance

Handmade Articles / Skill Development

Promoting traditional handicrafts, artisan skill development, and market placement support for home-based craftswomen and youth.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Handicraft & artisan training sessions
  • Quality enhancement & product design support
  • Exhibition & market linkages for women creators
