ABOUT SULOCHANA FOUNDATION

Sulochana Foundation is a Mumbai-based non-profit organization incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, as a company limited by guarantee. Founded on secular, non-political, and charitable principles, the Foundation applies all its surplus resources directly toward public welfare and societal upliftment across India, operating with complete transparency and a strict prohibition on dividend distribution to members.

OUR MISSION

At the core of its mission is the sustainable advancement of marginalized communities through inclusive education, skill development, and women's empowerment. The Foundation works to establish institutions ranging from digital learning centers and vocational hubs to community healthcare clinics and shelter homes. Key initiatives focus on fostering livelihood generation for women and youth, promoting preventive and maternal healthcare, and supporting vulnerable groups, including children and senior citizens.

ANIMAL WELFARE & ENVIRONMENT

Beyond social development, Sulochana Foundation is committed to compassionate animal welfare and environmental sustainability. It actively works to set up rescue centers, veterinary facilities, vaccination programs, and shelter services for domestic and stray animals. The organization partners with local communities, institutions, and CSR stakeholders to foster a compassionate and resilient society.
