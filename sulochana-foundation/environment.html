ECO-SUSTAINABILITY & ANIMAL CARE

Environmental Initiatives & Conservation

Driving tree plantation drives, environmental cleanliness campaigns, eco-awareness workshops, and compassionate animal welfare initiatives.

Tree Plantation

Executing sapling plantation drives, urban greening projects, and tree maintenance across urban and rural pockets.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Native sapling plantation drives
  • Community garden & urban greening initiatives
  • Long-term tree care & protection awareness

Environmental Awareness

Conducting workshops on waste segregation, plastic reduction, water conservation, and eco-friendly community lifestyles.

Key Focus Areas:
  • School & community eco-workshops
  • Single-use plastic reduction campaigns
  • Rainwater harvesting & water saving awareness

Cleanliness Drives

Organizing Swachh Bharat cleanliness drives in public spaces, parks, and beach areas with active volunteer participation.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Public area & park sanitation drives
  • Waste management & segregation awareness
  • Community cleanliness volunteer events

Nature Conservation

Promoting biodiversity preservation, eco-system restoration, animal shelter support, and sustainable environmental practices.

Key Focus Areas:
  • Local ecosystem & habitat protection
  • Biodiversity awareness walks & sessions
  • Support for domestic & stray animal shelters
